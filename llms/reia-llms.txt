# Real Estate Institute of Australia (REIA)

> The national federation of Australia's eight state and territory Real Estate Institutes, founded in 1924
> and based in Canberra. REIA is a policy, research and accreditation body — it administers the REIA
> National Principles of Conduct, awards Associate (AREI) and Fellow accreditation, runs the Australasian
> Auctioneering Championships and the National Awards for Excellence, makes submissions to the Commonwealth
> Government, and publishes the quarterly Housing Affordability Report (HAR) and Real Estate Market Facts
> (REMF) series. It does not operate market infrastructure: Australian listings run through REA Group and
> Domain, settlement through PEXA, valuation through PropTrack and CoreLogic, and title through the state
> land registries.

## API surface

REIA publishes **no API**. There is no developer portal, no API subdomain, no OpenAPI/Swagger, no OData
`$metadata`, no GraphQL, no AsyncAPI, no webhooks, no SDKs, no Postman collection, no MCP server and no
`/.well-known/` documents. RESO — the United States listing-data standard mandated by NAR — has no
Australian counterpart and appears nowhere on the REIA estate; no Australian organisation appears in the
public RESO certificate directory. What REIA sells is the data itself as paid document subscriptions, not
machine access to it. See `review.yml` in this repository for the full probe record.

- [Probe record (review.yml)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/reia/refs/heads/main/review.yml): every subdomain, contract path, feed and `/.well-known/` path probed, with HTTP status.
- [Well-known index](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/reia/refs/heads/main/well-known/reia-well-known.yml): the `/.well-known/` discovery surface — all 404.
- [Domain security](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/reia/refs/heads/main/security/reia-domain-security.yml): TLS, HSTS, DNSSEC, CAA, SPF and DMARC posture for reia.com.au.

## Research and data (paid, document-delivered)

- [Research overview](https://www.reia.com.au/research): the HAR and REMF quarterly series.
- [Housing Affordability Report (HAR)](https://www.reia.com.au/research/har): flagship report subscription plus seven separately-priced underlying datasets.
- [Real Estate Market Facts (REMF)](https://www.reia.com.au/research/remf): flagship report subscription plus fifteen separately-priced underlying datasets.
- [Standalone research reports](https://www.reia.com.au/standalone-research): free PDFs — Build to Rent, Property Management Action Plan, CRE outlooks.
- [Subscriber sign-in](https://www.reia.com.au/sign-in): report and dataset account login. Not a developer portal.
- [Create an account](https://www.reia.com.au/create-account): registration plus the Website Terms and Conditions, whose clause 6 forbids reproduction, distribution or resale of content without written permission and requires attribution to REIA.

## Standards, conduct and accreditation

- [REIA National Principles of Conduct](https://www.reia.com.au/conduct): the principles state institute codes and training are built on.
- [Associate (AREI) and Fellow accreditation](https://www.reia.com.au/accredit): criteria, application forms and fees; requires state or territory institute endorsement.
- [Member, affiliate, partner and alliance network](https://www.reia.com.au/our-network): the eight state and territory institutes, plus REINZ, FIABCI, NAR and ASBEC.

## Policy and advocacy

- [Submissions to government](https://www.reia.com.au/submissions): free PDFs.
- [AML/CTF Tranche 2](https://www.reia.com.au/aml-ctf): obligations for real estate professionals from 1 July 2026.
- [Residential energy efficiency](https://www.reia.com.au/residential-energy-efficiency): the Home Energy Ratings Disclosure Framework and NatHERS.
